# US AI K–12 State Guidance Project

Analysis of US state K–12 AI guidance documents: what states prioritize, what explains
those priorities, and where their guidance comes from.

## Research questions
- **RQ1** — How do the states compare in their governance priorities in terms of topics
  and audiences?
- **RQ2** — Are state characteristics (demographics, fiscal/infrastructural capacity,
  political leaning) indicative of patterns in state priorities in the guidance documents?
- **RQ3** — Which epistemic sources do states rely upon and share, including peer states?

## Layout
```
data/              shared datasets (corpus, covariates, extracted sources)
analysis/
  RQ1/             governance priorities: topics & audiences
  RQ2/             state characteristics vs. priorities
  RQ3/             epistemic sources & cross-state sharing
```
Each folder has its own README. See `analysis/<RQ>/README.md` for details.
